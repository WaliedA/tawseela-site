Talk to us How the platform works

Duty of care

The question is never where the bus is. It is whether the people on it are safe.

Tracking tells you a vehicle's position. Tawseela tells you who boarded, who did not, whether the driver is fit to drive, and whether the route being driven is the one that was approved.

Passenger manifest

Boarding and alighting against the roster, per stop. Missing and unexpected passengers raise an alert while the bus is still on the route.

Route and pickup adherence

Planned versus actual for every trip: stops served, times kept, deviations explained and recorded.

Driver fatigue and fitness

Working hours, rest, licence and compliance status enforced in the roster, with in-cab driver monitoring where fitted.

An auditable record

Immutable events and provenance for every observation. When a parent, an employer or a regulator asks, the answer exists.

The operating problem

One plan, one record, from roster to report

Most operations run the plan in spreadsheets and the vehicles in a vendor portal. Tawseela holds both, so a change to a driver, a route or a passenger list is reflected everywhere at once.

Why an operator offers it

Fewer cars in the car park, fuller buses, and an emissions number you can defend

Transport accounts for roughly a fifth to a quarter of global greenhouse gas emissions. For an employer or a school, the daily commute is the largest emissions line they can actually influence.

Modes in one plan

Your shuttle and staff lines, public transit from GTFS, walking, cycling and ride-hail as legs of one journey

Decide with data

ETA, fare, CO2 and weather for every option, so the greener choice is also the convenient one

Carbon credits per trip

Avoided emissions calculated per journey and credited to the commuter; redeemable with partners you choose

Same record as operations

Demand from the app feeds line planning; occupancy and CO2 flow into the operator's reports and ESG disclosures

Emissions, measured

CO2 per trip from distance and vehicle class, not from estimates

The same model has been run on a full city GTFS plan, aggregating 81,000 daily trips and assigning emissions by vehicle type, and on 10 million kilometres of real commuter travel.

Four commitments, written into the architecture

Vendor neutral

Replace a telematics engine, a hardware vendor, a solver or an AI model without rewriting the domain. Your data and your identifiers stay yours.

Human-approved automation

Optimisation produces candidate plans. Nothing changes a roster, a route or a trip without validation and an approval you control.

Provenance on everything

Every externally sourced fact carries where it came from, when it was observed and how confident it is. Events are never rewritten.

Electric and autonomous ready

Energy state, charging and autonomy capability are attributes of the same domain, not a separate product to buy later.

Commercial model

Software as a service, priced per vehicle

  • Annual subscription per vehicle, inclusive of connectivity, with volume tiers as your fleet grows
  • Planning, operations, compliance and reporting included; optimisation and intelligence as add-on modules
  • Works with your existing tracking hardware where compatible; installed bundles available for new vehicles
  • Your data, your identity provider, your tenancy. No revenue share, no marketplace, no resale of your capacity

Security and tenancy

Your operation is a tenant. Nothing crosses the line without your policy.

  • Your own identity provider; grants provisioned per user, no shared credentials
  • Tenant-scoped data, versioned read policies, encrypted sessions
  • Durable approvals and audit trail for every action, human or agent
  • Shadow-first onboarding: your live data is captured read-only, reconciled and signed off before anything is promoted
